Today I learned about Tuples. Tuples are similar to lists in that they store data, but what makes a tuple different is it is immutable, like strings.

To assign a Tuple values, the following syntax can be used:

uno, dos = 1, 2

which will then be printed as follows:
    uno, dos
    (1, 2)
You can also call one specific value:
    uno
    1
    dos
    2
You can also make a tuple out of a string, using the split function
    email = jpaglieri1@babson.edu 
    username, domain = email.split("@")

    username
    jpaglieri1

    domain
    babson.edu

Values can also be returned as a tupe. For example, the funciton divmod creates a tuple by dividing two numbers and than creating a tuple containing the quotient and the remainder.
